About This App

All practical work in the course is designed as a single end-to-end project, where each subsequent task logically continues and expands on the previous one. This sequential approach allows you to go through the full development cycle step by step, from creating the initial structure to implementing complex functionality, such as working with the server and authentication. Thus, the end result of your work during the semester will be a complete, functional, and ready-to-demo web application developed on the Angular framework.
